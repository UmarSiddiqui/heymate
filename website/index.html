Try a task you want to learn.

Open a desktop app or website, hold Control + Option, and ask: “Walk me through this, one step at a time.” HeyMate shows a plan, points to the first action, and checks the screen again when you click the target or say “next.”

Small apps for the space you never used.

Everything you'd want from a notch app: Now Playing with album art and a live visualizer, a File Shelf with AirDrop, your next event, battery, volume and brightness HUDs, Bluetooth devices, and a camera mirror. Plus focus timers, clipboard history, and downloads. Turn on only what you want.

Help that stays close to your work.

Learn by doing.

Hold ⌃⌥ and ask how to use the desktop app or website in front of you. Hear the explanation and follow the pointer. During a walkthrough, HeyMate captures fresh screen context between steps. You can correct a step or stop at any time.

claude codex opencode

It runs on your subscription.

Sign in through the official Claude Code or Codex CLI. HeyMate adds no subscription fee. Your provider's plan limits still apply; optional API and voice services can have their own charges.

It asks before it acts.

Coding agents show a read-only plan before execution. Approved jobs get workspace snapshots for undo, and connected tools have approval rules for external actions. Undo covers local files, not messages already sent or remote changes.

Mates

A familiar mate for each job.

Create a patient design tutor from the Mates view. Choose a name, a job, a personality, and a face. Give your mate a workspace and, when useful, a routine. Start with one clear job and shape how it helps you.

  • Personality and notes. Set a personality prompt and saved notes for each mate.
  • Routines. Daily at 9, or every 4 hours. Results land in the mate's chat, even while you're elsewhere.
  • Faces. Twenty built-in, or drop in your own.

Coding agents

Real work. With a leash.

Know what your mate can see.

Screen context is used for active interactions that need it, including new captures between walkthrough steps. Enabled routines, standing orders, and approved jobs can run in the background.

On your disk.

Chat history, memory, mates, and routines live in ~/Library/Application Support/heymate/. HeyMate chat history does not contain screenshot or audio bytes.

On your Mac.

Credentials are stored locally and used with services you connect. Questions and screen context go to your selected AI provider.

Capture controls.

Password managers and System Settings are excluded by default. Some integrations use temporary screenshot files; see the privacy notes.

Invisible on calls.

One switch hides the notch and cursor from screen shares.

What's new.

HeyMate checks for updates on its own. Here's what the latest releases changed.

  1. 1.0.44Oct 12, 2026
    • Chats and Agents, side by side. The window's sidebar now has two tabs: Chats for your own conversations with HeyMate, and Agents for everyone else. New chat always starts fresh with HeyMate.
    • Meet a cast. Answer a few questions and HeyMate suggests three agents for the work you do, each with its own face. New face packs (Signal, Margin, and Instrument) join the originals, and your existing agents keep the faces they have.
    • Talk to a working agent from the notch. Mates are now called agents everywhere, and you can send one a follow-up from the notch while it's still working.
  2. 1.0.42Oct 12, 2026
    • The window hangs from the notch. HeyMate's window now drops out of the notch on a narrow neck and folds away when you switch apps. Detach it to get a normal window, and attach it back with one click.
    • An inbox for your mates. The sidebar shows each mate's latest message and when it arrived. Each mate has a profile with a big portrait, its routines, and its files as thumbnails, and an empty chat leads with a big Hold to talk button.
    • Dictation learns your words. Fix a word HeyMate typed for you and it hears that word right next time. Learned words are listed, and can be cleared, in Settings › Talk & Voice.
    • Pick a voice by ear. Choose from ten on-device voices by tapping to hear each one, and set how fast HeyMate talks (0.75× to 1.5×).
    • Small things that add up. Esc stops a reply, talking keeps working when you switch microphones or AirPods connect, screenshots no longer pick HeyMate's invisible overlay, jobs play a sound when they start, need you, or finish, and shortcuts show as key caps.
  3. 1.0.41Oct 11, 2026
    • Ctrl + Option works again. HeyMate now asks for Input Monitoring, which newer macOS needs before an app can hear a shortcut. Once you allow it, the shortcuts start working right away, no restart needed.

Install HeyMate

Download the DMG below, or inspect the installer before running this command in Terminal.

curl -fsSL getheymate.vercel.app/install.sh | bash

Or with Homebrew:

brew install umarsiddiqui/tap/heymate

Or download the DMG. Current builds are self-signed and not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks the first launch, review the app and use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway if you choose to proceed.

It will ask for Microphone, Accessibility, and Screen Recording. Here's exactly why.

Bring the AI you already have.

Claude or ChatGPT: click sign in and finish in your browser. HeyMate sets up the rest. Or use OpenCode with free, local, or other models, or your own API key for an Anthropic-compatible server.

Apple Intelligence is an optional text-only brain on supported Macs running macOS 26 or later. It cannot see the screen. Screen guidance needs a vision-capable provider.

Hold ⌃⌥ and say hey.
