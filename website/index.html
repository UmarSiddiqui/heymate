Three things it does that nothing else on your Mac does.

It sees what you see.

Hold ⌃⌥ and ask about whatever is in front of you: an error, a chart, a form, a slow Mac. HeyMate looks at your screen only for that question, answers out loud, and points its cursor at what it means.

claude codex opencode

It runs on your subscription.

Pick Claude or ChatGPT, click sign in, done. HeyMate uses the plan you already pay for through its official app, so there's no HeyMate account and no second bill. Ever.

It asks before it acts.

Agents plan read-only first. Nothing is written, clicked, or sent without an approval card. Every change is snapshotted so you can undo.

Mates

Not one assistant. A small team.

Say "make me a mate that tracks my YouTube comments" and HeyMate creates one — with a name, a job, a personality, and a face. Give it a folder to work in. Give it a schedule. It'll report back in its own chat.

  • Souls. Each mate has its own personality prompt and its own memory.
  • Routines. Daily at 9, or every 4 hours. Results land in the mate's chat, even while you're elsewhere.
  • Faces. Twenty built-in, or drop in your own.

Coding agents

Real work. With a leash.

Nothing runs in the background.

HeyMate only takes a screenshot when you press the hotkey, and screenshots are never stored.

On your disk.

Install HeyMate

Paste this in Terminal. No Mac security warning.

curl -fsSL getheymate.vercel.app/install.sh | bash

Or with Homebrew:

brew install umarsiddiqui/tap/heymate

Or download the DMG. If macOS says it can't verify it, open System Settings > Privacy > Security and click Open Anyway.

It will ask for Microphone, Accessibility, and Screen Recording. Here's exactly why.

Pick the AI you already pay for.

Claude or ChatGPT. Click sign in and finish in your browser. HeyMate sets up the rest.

No plan? Choose Neither and it runs on Apple Intelligence on your Mac. Already signed in to the Claude, Codex, or OpenCode CLI? HeyMate uses that.
