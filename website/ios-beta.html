How to get started

Requires iOS 17 or later and HeyMate 1.0.97 or later on your Mac.

  1. Request access. Once enrolled, use the TestFlight invitation to install HeyMate on your iPhone.
  2. Sign in with the same Google account on iPhone and Mac. On your Mac, open HeyMate Settings → Devices → iPhone companion and show a pairing code.
  3. Scan the code in the iPhone app. Keep HeyMate open on an awake, online Mac while you use the companion.
