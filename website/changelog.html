Changelog

What's new.

Every HeyMate release, newest first. HeyMate checks for new releases on its own. Commit-level notes are on GitHub Releases.

  1. 1.0.35Oct 10, 2026
    • A chat window that makes sense. Click a mate anywhere on its row to open the chat, and use the pencil to edit it. Apps and Settings are labeled in the sidebar, and the chat header sits up beside the window buttons.
    • Choose apps for a chat in one place. The Apps button next to the message box lists every connected app with its logo and an on/off switch, and stays open while you pick.
    • Real logos. Claude, ChatGPT, and your connected apps now show their own icons in Settings and the chat.
    • Chats open on the newest reply. The latest answer no longer hides under the message box, and a Latest button brings you back down after scrolling up.
    • Calmer messages, easier buttons. Copy, edit, regenerate, and delete appear as icons when you point at a message. Buttons are easier to hit, and headings in replies read cleanly.
  2. 1.0.33Oct 10, 2026
    • Settings titles tuck away as you scroll. On long Settings pages the section name moves into a slim bar at the top.
    • Reduce Motion is respected. With Reduce Motion on, Settings fades between pages instead of jumping.
  3. 1.0.32Oct 10, 2026
    • A calmer Settings. Eight clear sections, from General to Privacy & Data, in a sidebar that works in light and dark.
    • Find any setting by name. Press ⌘F, type what you're after, and HeyMate jumps to that setting and highlights it.
    • Shortcut clashes are caught. If two shortcuts share the same keys, both show a warning, and Restore defaults puts them back.
    • Nothing is erased without asking. Signing out, removing keys, and erasing data always ask first.
  4. 1.0.31Oct 10, 2026
    • HeyMate points one step at a time. Ask how something works and the cursor flies to each part in turn, saying and showing that step before it moves on.
    • Walkthroughs that remember where you are. Ask "show me how to…" and HeyMate guides the first step, waits for you to click it, takes a fresh look, and guides the next until you're done. Press Escape to stop.
    • Captions you can actually read. The text beside the cursor now stays while HeyMate is speaking and fades a moment after it finishes, instead of vanishing early.
    • "How do I…" questions see your screen. HeyMate now looks at your screen for how-to questions, so it can point at the right button.
  5. 1.0.30Oct 10, 2026
    • A smoother on-device voice. Replies no longer pause for nearly a second between sentences, so HeyMate sounds like it's talking, not reading a list.
    • No wait on the first reply. The on-device voice now gets ready when HeyMate opens, so your first answer starts speaking right away instead of after several seconds.
  6. 1.0.29Oct 10, 2026
    • Talk starts the instant you press. The mic turns on the moment the shortcut goes down, so your first words are no longer cut off while HeyMate connects.
    • Your installed app stays put. Opening the Permissions card no longer replaces the HeyMate in your Applications folder.
  7. 1.0.28Oct 9, 2026
    • "What am I looking at?" just works. Questions about what's in front of you now always include your screen, and a quick "hi" no longer drags the chat back to your last topic.
    • Push-to-talk never gets stuck. If macOS misses the moment you let go of the shortcut, HeyMate notices and stops listening anyway.
    • Easier permission setup. The whole HeyMate.app card in Permissions is now a drag handle, and dragging it into System Settings works on the first try.
    • A smoother notch. The card opens with a softer, liquid motion, connector suggestions show each app's real logo, and Quit now sits in the top-right corner.
  8. 1.0.27Oct 9, 2026
    • Connected apps come back on their own. If HeyMate's link to Composio was lost while your key and connected apps were still saved, it reconnects at launch, so mates can read your Gmail and Calendar again instead of starting a job that opens the Mail app.
  9. 1.0.26Oct 9, 2026
    • Email questions answered in chat. Ask a mate about your inbox or calendar and it uses your connected Gmail and Google Calendar right away, instead of starting a slow job that opens the Mail app.
    • No duplicate jobs. If a mate's plan is waiting for your approval and you ask again, it reminds you to approve it instead of starting a second job.
  10. 1.0.25Oct 9, 2026
    • No more password prompts after updates. Saved keys (Composio, ElevenLabs, custom API, app connectors) now live in a private file only your Mac account can read, instead of the Keychain. The first launch moves your existing keys over, which may ask one last time.
    • A quieter notch. The shortcut labels and unread-count badges are gone. Mates with something new show a small dot instead.
  11. 1.0.23Oct 9, 2026
    • Mates use app control. With Computer control on and the Cua driver installed, asking a mate to do something in another app now starts a job for it, instead of the mate saying it can't.
  12. 1.0.22Oct 9, 2026
    • Talk no longer gets stuck. A press that heard nothing, or that you let go of too quickly, could leave HeyMate showing Listening until you restarted it. It now goes straight back to ready.
    • Steadier status. The notch no longer flickers while the microphone starts, and keeps showing that it's working while your words are being transcribed.
  13. 1.0.20Oct 9, 2026
    • Conversations that flow. With Claude or Codex answering, a chat now keeps one live session, so follow-ups pick up where you left off instead of starting cold. Replies appear word by word as they're written.
    • Background app control. Turn on Computer control in Settings and press Install to add Cua's open-source driver. Approved jobs can then work in other apps in the background, without taking your cursor or focus.
    • Still your call. App control only runs inside a plan you approved. Jobs can never read or change your clipboard, reconfigure the driver, or install anything.
  14. 1.0.19Oct 9, 2026
    • A private voice, on your Mac. Download the on-device voice once (about 600 MB) and HeyMate listens with Parakeet and talks with Kokoro, all on your Mac, offline and free. It's offered during setup and in Settings, and needs an Apple Silicon Mac.
    • Bring your own ElevenLabs. Paste your ElevenLabs API key in Settings to use ElevenLabs for listening, with your words appearing live as you talk, and for speaking. The free plan works.
    • Never left silent. If ElevenLabs or the on-device voice can't run, HeyMate falls back to your Mac's voice for that turn. The Mac voice now picks the best voice you have installed and skips the novelty ones.
    • Simpler voice options. AssemblyAI and OpenAI transcription are gone. If you had picked one, HeyMate moves you to ElevenLabs, or to your Mac if you haven't added a key.
  15. 1.0.18Oct 6, 2026
    • One friendly ask. After HeyMate has answered five of your questions, it offers once to open the GitHub page so you can star it. Say "Not now" and it never asks again.
  16. 1.0.17Oct 3, 2026
    • Agents can add mates. When a plan you approve calls for new mates, the job now creates them, each with its own chat and folder.
    • Honest results. A job that couldn't finish now shows as Failed with a reason, not "Work completed".
    • Approve plan works in big projects. Build and dependency folders no longer count toward the undo size limit, and if approval can't start you'll see why next to the button.
  17. 1.0.16Oct 3, 2026
    • Maintenance build. No visible changes.
  18. 1.0.15Oct 3, 2026
    • Faster Talk with Codex. HeyMate keeps Codex warm, so spoken answers start sooner.
  19. 1.0.14Oct 2, 2026
    • Gmail and Google Calendar, no extra sign-in. HeyMate reads them through the accounts already on your Mac.
    • Faster Talk with Claude. Answers come from a Claude Code process that's already running.
  20. 1.0.13Oct 2, 2026
    • One-click sign-in. Connect Claude or ChatGPT during setup without opening Terminal.
    • Simpler layout. The app comes down to three places: Chat, Apps, and Settings.
    • Settings is split into General, Accounts, Notch, Privacy, and Advanced tabs.
  21. 1.0.12Oct 2, 2026
    • Silent mode is easier to reach, and its replies read better on screen.
  22. 1.0.11Oct 2, 2026
    • Silent mode. Type to HeyMate instead of talking, and read the answer instead of hearing it.
  23. 1.0.10Oct 1, 2026
    • Mates now reliably start agent work when you ask them to.
  24. 1.0.9Oct 1, 2026

  25. 1.0.8Oct 1, 2026

    • A mate opens with one click even when the window isn't focused.
    • The notch brings plan approvals into view and counts failed jobs.
    • Background agents work with Codex installed through npm.
