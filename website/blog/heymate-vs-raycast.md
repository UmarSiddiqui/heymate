# HeyMate vs Raycast: which Mac AI workflow fits you?

Compare HeyMate and Raycast for guided learning, AI subscriptions, agents, and daily workflows. Includes official sources and reasons to choose either.

By the HeyMate project. Published 2026-11-01. Category: Comparisons.

> Consider HeyMate when you want an open-source desktop companion, spoken screen guidance, and specialist mates without a HeyMate subscription. Consider Raycast when its integrated AI, command interface, and extension ecosystem fit your existing workflow. Both support existing Claude or ChatGPT subscriptions.

## Where the products overlap

Raycast’s current [AI product page](https://www.raycast.com/core-features/ai) describes screen awareness, custom agents, projects, automations, and extensions. Its [subscription guide](https://manual.raycast.com/ai/bring-your-own-subscription) documents using Claude or ChatGPT through the provider’s CLI, with a paid Raycast plan. Usage goes against that provider subscription rather than Raycast AI credits.

That overlap matters: bringing your own AI subscription is not unique to HeyMate. Choose based on the interaction you want, the tools you already use, and the total cost of your setup. This is a project-published comparison based on documentation checked November 1, 2026, not a hands-on speed or accuracy benchmark.

## Compare the decision points

| Question | HeyMate | Raycast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How do I interact? | Push-to-talk, spoken guidance, a desktop pointer, and specialist mates. | AI chat, commands, agents, and extension-driven workflows; see the AI overview linked above. |
| Can I use an existing subscription? | Claude Code, Codex, and other configured routes; provider limits apply. | Claude or ChatGPT via CLI on a paid Raycast plan, per its subscription guide. |
| What do I pay for the app? | MIT licensed; no HeyMate subscription. Optional services can charge. | Raycast AI is part of paid Pro plans; check current terms rather than assuming a fixed price. |
| What should I verify? | Provider vision support, required permissions, and current installer requirements. | Plan access, current system requirements, supported models, and required extensions. |

Raycast plan and feature statements above come from its [official AI overview](https://www.raycast.com/core-features/ai). HeyMate requirements and signing details are documented in the [project README](https://github.com/UmarSiddiqui/heymate).

## When HeyMate is worth trying

Try HeyMate if your question is often “what am I looking at, and what should I try next?” Its guided-learning pattern lets you perform the task while the assistant explains the current step. A named mate can also keep a recurring role and habits, while optional notch tools keep small utilities nearby.

HeyMate supports macOS 14.2 or later on Apple silicon and Intel; screen guidance still needs a vision-capable provider. Builds are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple, and there is no HeyMate subscription.

## When Raycast may be the better choice

Choose Raycast if the workflow described in its documentation already matches how you work and the paid plan fits your budget. There is little reason to switch a working tool solely to obtain a feature both products offer. A launcher-centered routine and a companion-centered routine may also coexist rather than compete for every task.

Avoid judging either tool from a model name alone. Give each the same sample task, verify the output, and record how much setup and correction you needed. That produces evidence about your use case instead of an unsupported claim that one product is universally better.

## A practical comparison you can run

1. Use a non-sensitive sample task in an app you already know.
2. Ask for an explanation, one next step, and a way to verify the result.
3. Repeat with the same provider where feasible, noting any differences in tool access.
4. Compare the result, interaction, account permissions, and total ongoing cost.
