# Creator and YouTuber outreach email

**Who to send it to:** creators who teach Mac apps, productivity or creative software, and who test on macOS. Pick 5 per week and personalise each one. A sentence that shows you watched a specific video is required; never bulk-send. If you ever offer anything of value, the creator must disclose it, so say so.

Don't include sponsorship talk, a request for a positive review, or attachments. Send from your own address. You send it; Claude doesn't.

---

**Subject:** Free Mac app idea for a video: ask about anything on screen, it points

```
Hi [first name],

I liked your [specific video title] — especially [one concrete moment, e.g. the part where you showed …].

I build HeyMate, a free, open-source Mac app that lives in the notch. You hold Control+Option, ask out loud about anything on your screen, and it answers by voice and points at the spot. It runs on the viewer's own Claude or ChatGPT plan, so there's nothing to buy.

I think it could fit a [their format, e.g. "hidden Mac apps" roundup / tutorial workflow] video. Here's a 20-second unedited recording: [Clip A link].

If you'd like to try it, I can send setup notes. It isn't notarized yet, so first launch needs one "Open Anyway" step. No obligation to cover it, and honest criticism is genuinely useful to me.

Thanks either way,
Umar
https://github.com/UmarSiddiqui/heymate
```

---

**Follow-up:** send one, only if you have something new, such as a new release with faces and cards. Wait 10 days or more. Otherwise don't follow up.

**Tracking:** keep a private list with name, date sent, video referenced, and reply. Don't scrape or compile personal contact details. Use only the business email a creator publishes on their channel's About page.
