## Maker first comment (post within a minute of going live)

```
Hey Product Hunt, I'm Umar, and I built HeyMate.

It started with a small annoyance. Every time something on screen confused me, I'd screenshot it, switch to a chat window, paste it, and explain what I was looking at. HeyMate turns that into one gesture: hold Control + Option, ask out loud, let go. It looks at the screen for that question, answers by voice, and moves a pointer to the thing it means.

A few things I cared about:
• Your plan, not mine. It signs in through the official Claude Code or Codex CLI, so your existing Claude or ChatGPT subscription does the work. There's no HeyMate subscription and no metered key.
• It asks before it acts. Helpers you create (describe one in a sentence) can run routines, but anything that sends, deletes, or pays waits for your yes.
• Private by default. The screen is captured only when you ask, chat history keeps text and not screenshots, and voice can run fully on-device.
• Open source. MIT licensed, native SwiftUI, macOS 14.2+.

Honest limits: it isn't notarized yet (no Apple Developer ID), so first launch needs "Open Anyway" or the Homebrew cask. And pointing is only as good as the vision model you use.

What's the first thing you'd ask it about on your screen?
```
